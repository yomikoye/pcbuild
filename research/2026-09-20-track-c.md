## Purchase gates — all must be closed before recommendation

No candidate above passes this checklist. Active-looking C1 has advertised model/quantity/price evidence but no complete identity, condition or fit validation; C3–C6 additionally fail availability.

1. Verify actual private/business seller identity and right to sell; obtain invoice or written ownership proof and both serial numbers/photos. Marketplace brand is not seller identity.
2. Prefer a business seller with at least 12-month written coverage and clear handler/returns/RMA; distinguish commercial warranty from Gewährleistung. Confirm Poland coverage, return address, costs and practical cross-border collection route. Private sale requires in-person inspection and testing.
3. Verify identical exact manufacturer model/SKU, PCB revision, cooler/block geometry and power connectors on both physical cards; confirm both available simultaneously in one transaction. No unrelated-listing pair construction.
4. Obtain disclosed mining, liquid exposure, corrosion, board repair, BIOS modification and fan history; document thermal-pad/paste replacements and original cooler inclusion. Reject unresolved artifacts, throttling, fan faults, damaged connectors, corrosion or liquid damage.
5. Run at least **30 minutes sustained CUDA or OCCT load plus VRAM testing**, recording GPU core, hotspot and GDDR6X memory-junction temperatures for each serial; validate sustained clocks, stability and no errors. Generic “tested”/FurMark marketing alone is insufficient.
6. Retain full Track A inherited platform and locked 128GB factory-matched 4x32GB ECC RDIMM. No AM5/gaming-platform substitution. Validate CPU/BIOS and exact memory compatibility/stability separately before a complete-system BUY NOW.
7. Validate actual Gigabyte TRX50 AI TOP slot positions/electrical lane allocation, M.2 interactions, both cards' dimensions and connector/cable clearance in **Phanteks Enthoo Elite Server PH-ES916E_BK02**, alongside AW420 radiator/fans. Thick cards are acceptable only with meaningful intake gap and validated direct GPU airflow; thin/blower form is an option, not a mandatory preference.
8. Validate exact PRIME PX-2200 PSU/cables, independent leads for every high-power card socket (four for a two-socket-per-card pair, with additional sockets addressed if applicable), no daisy chains, safe bends, proper EU mains, GPU mechanical supports and fan layout. Validate sustained thermals and preferably undervolting; do not substitute nominal power assumptions for actual model evidence.
9. For water blocks: design and price complete pump/reservoir/radiator/fittings/coolant loop, rear-memory cooling, maintenance and pressure/leak testing. A thin water-block body alone is not an installable cooling solution.
10. Use a safe transaction method only after gates pass; no advance bank transfer to an unverified private seller. Confirm invoice/VAT treatment, destination delivery and final payable amount; never treat unknown shipping as free.
11. If NVLink is added, close exact generation/spacing/connector-position and application-benefit gates above. It remains optional and outside base cost until then.
