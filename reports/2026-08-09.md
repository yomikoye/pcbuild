### Track B exact-product findings

#### Motherboard

- Exact product: GIGABYTE `X870E AORUS MASTER X3D ICE`, EAN `4719331876517`.
- Alternate Germany listed €495 including VAT and immediate availability; final Polish shipping and destination VAT require checkout verification.
- One CPU-fed GPU runs PCIe 5.0 x16.
- Populating `PCIEX16` and `PCIEX8` produces PCIe 5.0 x8/x8.
- The chipset physical x16 slot is only PCIe 4.0 x4.
- 9950X3D2 support requires BIOS F8 or newer. Prefer the newest stable BIOS rather than a letter-suffixed beta unless needed.

#### Memory

- The board supports four UDIMM sockets and up to 256GB; 2x64GB is structurally supported.
- Kingston `KF556C40BBK2-128` remains the current factory-matched 128GB price lead.
- Its previous ASUS-QVL status does not prove validation on this new GIGABYTE board. Exact GIGABYTE QVL or memory-vendor validation must be rechecked before buying.
- Start conservatively, use A2/B2 and stability-test after BIOS update.

#### M.2 topology

- `M2A_CPU`: PCIe 5.0 x4; use for the 9100 PRO.
- `M2B_CPU`: shares bandwidth with ASMedia USB4; if populated, both links operate at up to x2.
- `M2C_SB`: PCIe 4.0 x4; use for the 990 PRO.
- `M2D_SB`: PCIe 4.0 x4 but disables chipset `PCIEX4` when populated.
- `M2E_SB`: PCIe 4.0 x2.
- None of the documented M.2 connectors reduces the CPU GPU x8/x8 pair.

#### Cooler

- Exact selected variant: white full PANORAMA 360 non-ARGB `L-P360N-DS3M-G1W`, EAN `6977029650353`; do not substitute PANORAMA SE.
- Radiator 399.5x120x30mm plus 25mm fans gives a 55mm stack.
- HAVN permits a 65mm top stack without RAM overlap, giving about 10mm nominal margin.
- Top exhaust is preferred. The large 118x92x92mm pump/display must be dry-fitted against the motherboard's VRM heatsinks.
- TRYX advertises six years for the cooler and two years for the AMOLED display; obtain seller confirmation because Polish listings may show only 24 months.
- KANALI software/firmware and the curved AMOLED introduce more complexity than the previous conventional AIO.

#### Case and airflow

- Exact selected case: HAVN HS 420 VGPU White `HVN-CA-HS420-07`, EAN `4251442512156`.
- Caseking listed €289.90 and in stock; final Poland shipping/VAT needs checkout verification.
- Motherboard ATX dimensions fit.
- No fans are included.
- Planned layout: 3x140 bottom intake, 3x140 right intake, 2x140 rear exhaust and PANORAMA 3x120 top exhaust.
- PRIME PX-2200 is 210mm long against HAVN's 220mm PSU-body limit, leaving only 10mm nominal body margin. Modular-cable bend clearance must be physically checked.

### Critical Track B dual-GPU warning

The HS 420 VGPU stock configuration is designed for one vertical GPU:

1. It includes one four-slot vertical bracket and one PCIe 5.0 x16 riser.
2. It does not support two vertical GPUs.
3. A future second GPU requires removing the VGPU assembly and installing both cards horizontally.
4. Two two-slot Founders Edition cards may be mechanically plausible after exact slot/airflow verification.
5. Two 2.5-slot ProArt cards consume essentially all six horizontal case slots and are not an airflow-safe default.

Therefore the 2,200W B1 configuration is **dual-GPU-power-ready**, but Track B is not chassis-ready for two GPUs in its selected stock layout. The 68,195.41 PLN figure is a theoretical hardware total, not a purchase-approved two-GPU build.

## Architecture advantages and compromises

### Threadripper advantages

- More PCIe connectivity and stronger expansion topology
- Better multi-GPU chassis/platform strategy
- Quad-channel ECC RDIMM
- Higher future memory capacity
- Stronger long-term workstation flexibility

### AM5 advantages

- Lower platform price and CPU power
- Better gaming-focused CPU
- White showcase aesthetic
- Curved AMOLED cooler display
- VGPU presentation for one RTX 5090
- Substantial 128GB system memory

### AM5 compromises

- Dual-channel non-ECC UDIMM
- Two CPU-fed GPUs operate x8/x8 rather than x16/x16
- Selected HAVN VGPU layout is one-GPU-oriented
- Fewer total PCIe lanes
- M.2 sharing with USB4/chipset expansion as documented above
- Weaker future memory expansion
