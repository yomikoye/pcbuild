## Architecture advantages / compromises

**Threadripper:** more PCIe connectivity, superior multi-GPU platform, ECC RDIMM, quad-channel memory, higher memory capacity and stronger long-term expansion; much higher uncertain platform cost and power. **AM5:** lower platform price/power, better gaming-focused CPU, cheaper motherboard/RAM/case, white showcase design and substantial 128GB. Compromises: dual-channel, preferred 128GB, x8/x8 electrical dual GPU, fewer PCIe lanes, chipset/USB4 M.2 sharing, weaker 256GB+ expansion and HAVN VGPU not two-GPU-ready in stock layout. **C:** Threadripper expansion with potentially lower GPU acquisition cost, 48GB aggregate physical VRAM and the option of one explicitly distributed model or two independent 24GB workloads; used-card condition/warranty risk, separate memory devices, software/communication overhead, ~700W heat, older Ampere Tensor/RT/media and incomplete fit-validated matched-pair evidence. NVLink optional only, not VRAM pooling.
