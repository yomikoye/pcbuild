## Architecture advantages and compromises

### Threadripper advantages

- More PCIe connectivity and a superior multi-GPU platform.
- ECC RDIMM and quad-channel memory.
- Higher memory capacity and stronger long-term expansion.
- A workstation-first case/slot strategy better suited to horizontal multi-GPU operation than stock HAVN VGPU.

### AM5 advantages

- Lower platform price and power and a better gaming-focused CPU.
- Cheaper motherboard, RAM and case.
- White showcase design and substantial 128GB capacity.

### AM5 compromises

- Dual-channel memory and a preferred 128GB 2x64GB configuration.
- Two CPU-fed GPU slots operate PCIe 5.0 **x8/x8**, not x16/x16.
- Fewer PCIe lanes, chipset/USB4 M.2 sharing and weaker 256GB+ expansion.
- `M2B_CPU` shares bandwidth with ASMedia USB4; `M2D_SB` disables chipset `PCIEX4`; `M2E_SB` is PCIe 4.0 x2.
- The selected HAVN VGPU chassis is not two-GPU-ready in its stock layout.

### Track C advantages and compromises

- Threadripper PCIe/expansion platform, 48GB aggregate physical VRAM, lower acquisition cost and the choice of one software-distributed model or two independent 24GB workloads.
- Used-card condition/warranty risk, separate VRAM devices, multi-GPU communication/software overhead and approximately 700W nominal GPU heat.
- Older Ampere Tensor/RT/media features; one RTX 5090 remains faster and simpler when the workload fits 32GB.
- Current fit-validated matching-pair evidence is incomplete. This week's water-block pair adds custom-loop design, leak and maintenance risk.

- **AIO/case:** PANORAMA's 30mm radiator plus 25mm fans gives a 55mm top-exhaust stack under HAVN's 65mm no-RAM-overlap allowance; dry-fit the 118 × 92 × 92mm pump/display against VRM heatsinks. AW420 mounts top exhaust in Tracks A/C.
- **HAVN:** stock VGPU is a one-vertical-GPU layout. Two GPUs require VGPU removal and horizontal cards. Two 304 × 140 × 50mm ProArts consume the six horizontal-slot envelope and are not an airflow-safe default.
- **Track C geometry/cooling:** card thickness is conditional, not a ranking shortcut. The current water blocks are thin but unusable without a complete loop. Air-cooled thick pairs remain acceptable only with verified TRX50 slot placement, meaningful intake gap, direct side airflow and sustained thermal results.
- **Power/cables:** exact PX-2200 EAN `4711173878414` is ATX 3.1/PCIe 5.1 with native 12V-2x6. For dual 3090 use four independent 8-pin leads, no daisy chains. For 5090 fully seat the native cable and preserve bend radius. The 210mm PX body leaves only 10mm nominal body allowance in HAVN before cable space.
- **EU mains:** the 2200W unit is 220–240V-only at full specification; use a correctly earthed dedicated 230V circuit and avoid adapters/overloaded strips.
- **Fans/support:** Tracks A/C use 4x140 front intake, 2x140 rear exhaust, 6x120 side GPU intake and AW420 top exhaust. Track B uses 3x140 bottom + 3x140 side intake, 2x140 rear exhaust and PANORAMA top exhaust. Support every heavy GPU mechanically.
