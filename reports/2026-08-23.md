## Architecture advantages and compromises

### Threadripper advantages

- More PCIe connectivity and a superior multi-GPU platform.
- ECC RDIMM and quad-channel memory.
- Higher memory capacity and stronger long-term expansion.
- The Enthoo Elite Server strategy is materially better suited to horizontal multi-GPU cooling than the stock HAVN VGPU layout.

### AM5 advantages

- Lower platform price and power.
- Better gaming-focused primary CPU.
- Cheaper motherboard, RAM and case.
- White showcase design and substantial 128GB capacity.

### AM5 compromises

- Dual-channel memory and a preferred 128GB 2x64GB configuration.
- CPU-fed GPU slots run PCIe 5.0 **x16 with one card or x8/x8 with two**, never x16/x16 with two.
- Fewer PCIe lanes, chipset/USB4 M.2 sharing and weaker 256GB+ expansion.
- `M2B_CPU` shares bandwidth with ASMedia USB4; `M2D_SB` disables chipset `PCIEX4`; `M2E_SB` is PCIe 4.0 x2.
- The selected HAVN VGPU chassis is not two-GPU-ready in its stock layout.

### Track C advantages and compromises

- Inherits Threadripper's PCIe connectivity, ECC RDIMM, quad-channel memory and workstation-grade multi-GPU chassis strategy.
- Provides 48GB aggregate physical GPU memory at a much lower acquisition cost and can run one software-distributed model or two independent 24GB workloads.
- Used-card condition, private-sale warranty, separate VRAM devices, multi-GPU communication/software overhead and approximately 700W nominal GPU heat are material compromises.
- Ampere lacks Blackwell's newer FP4/FP8, Tensor, RT and media capabilities. One RTX 5090 remains faster and simpler for models that fit in 32GB.
- The provisional Zotac pair requires motherboard-slot, physical and sustained-thermal validation; thickness alone is not a rejection reason.

### Compatibility audit

- **CPU/BIOS:** Ryzen 9000 is supported. The 9950X3D2 needs BIOS F8 or newer; update to the latest stable BIOS before installation.
- **Memory:** Track A is a factory quad ECC RDIMM kit; Track B is a factory 2x64GB non-ECC UDIMM kit. Exact extractable `KF556C40BBK2-128` validation on this specific ICE board remains unresolved. Populate A2/B2, begin at DDR5-5600 and run long memory/stability tests. RDIMM is incompatible with AM5.
- **GPU lanes:** `PCIEX16` alone is PCIe 5.0 x16; two CPU-fed cards are **x8/x8**. M.2 population does not reduce that GPU pair.
- **M.2 placement:** put 9100 PRO in `M2A_CPU` and 990 PRO in `M2C_SB`. `M2B_CPU` shares with ASMedia USB4, `M2D_SB` disables chipset `PCIEX4`, and `M2E_SB` is PCIe 4.0 x2.
- **AIO/case:** mount the 30mm radiator + 25mm fans = 55mm PANORAMA stack at the top as exhaust, below HAVN's 65mm no-RAM-overlap limit. Dry-fit the 118x92x92mm pump/display against the VRM heatsinks.
- **HAVN GPU fit:** the stock VGPU assembly supports one vertical GPU. A second GPU requires its removal and two horizontal cards. Two 304x140x50mm 2.5-slot ProArt cards effectively occupy all six horizontal slots and are not an airflow-safe default.
- **PSU/cables:** exact PX-2200 EAN `4711173878414` is Seasonic ATX 3.1 with native 12V-2x6. Its 210mm body leaves only 10mm nominal room against HAVN's 220mm PSU-body allowance before modular-cable bending; verify native lead count, full connector seating and safe bend radius. B2's observed black VERTEX offer is explicitly ATX 3.0/EAN `4711173877721`; the ATX 3.1 White family is distinct and was not substituted.
- **Track C dual 3090:** PRIME PX-2200 is electrically sufficient; use four independent 8-pin leads and no daisy chains. Select card thickness from the verified slot layout and cooling result. Thick open-air cards are acceptable when they retain a meaningful intake gap; all candidates require slot-spacing, anti-sag and sustained CUDA/VRAM/temperature validation. NVLink requires the correct RTX 3090-generation bridge and exact physical spacing; it remains optional and excluded.
- **Mains:** sustained 2200W-class use needs a properly earthed dedicated 230V circuit appropriate to breaker/outlet limits; do not use adapters or overloaded strips.
- **Fans/support:** Track A uses 4x140 front intake, 2x140 rear exhaust, 6x120 side GPU intake and AW420 top exhaust. Track B uses 3x140 bottom + 3x140 side intake, 2x140 rear exhaust and PANORAMA top exhaust for slight positive pressure. Mechanically support heavy GPUs.
