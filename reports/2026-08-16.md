## Architecture advantages and compromises

### Threadripper advantages

- More PCIe connectivity.
- Superior multi-GPU platform.
- ECC RDIMM and quad-channel memory.
- Higher memory capacity and stronger long-term expansion.
- The Enthoo Elite Server strategy is materially better suited to horizontal multi-GPU cooling than the stock HAVN VGPU layout.

### AM5 advantages

- Lower platform price and power.
- Better gaming-focused primary CPU.
- Cheaper motherboard, RAM and case despite this week's RAM increase.
- White showcase design.
- Substantial 128GB capacity.

### AM5 compromises

- Dual-channel memory and a preferred 128GB 2x64GB configuration.
- CPU-fed GPU slots run PCIe 5.0 **x16 with one card or x8/x8 with two**, never x16/x16 with two.
- Fewer PCIe lanes.
- `M2B_CPU` shares bandwidth with ASMedia USB4; `M2D_SB` disables chipset `PCIEX4`; `M2E_SB` is PCIe 4.0 x2.
- Weaker 256GB+ expansion.
- The selected HAVN VGPU chassis is not two-GPU-ready in its stock layout.

### Compatibility audit

- **CPU/BIOS:** the selected board supports Ryzen 9000. The distinct 9950X3D2 requires BIOS F8 or newer; use the latest stable release, not an older board image.
- **Memory:** Track A kit is a factory quad ECC RDIMM kit validated for the TRX50 family; Track B is a factory 2x64GB UDIMM kit. Exact extractable `KF556C40BBK2-128` validation on this specific GIGABYTE ICE board remains unresolved, so start in A2/B2 at DDR5-5600 and stability-test. Never fit RDIMM to AM5.
- **GPU lanes:** `PCIEX16` alone is PCIe 5.0 x16; two CPU-fed cards are x8/x8. M.2 population does not reduce that GPU pair.
- **M.2 placement:** 9100 PRO in `M2A_CPU`; 990 PRO in `M2C_SB`. `M2B_CPU` shares with ASMedia USB4, `M2D_SB` disables chipset `PCIEX4`, and `M2E_SB` is PCIe 4.0 x2.
- **AIO/case:** mount the PANORAMA top as exhaust. Its 30mm radiator + 25mm fans = 55mm stack, below HAVN's 65mm top no-RAM-overlap limit. Dry-fit the 118x92x92mm pump/display against VRM heatsinks.
- **HAVN GPU fit:** stock VGPU hardware supports one vertical GPU. A second GPU requires removing it and fitting two horizontal cards. Two 304x140x50mm 2.5-slot ProArt cards occupy essentially all six horizontal slots and are not an airflow-safe default.
- **PSU:** PX-2200 exact EAN is the Seasonic ATX 3.1 unit with native 12V-2x6 capability. It is 210mm long against the HAVN 220mm PSU-body allowance, leaving only 10mm nominal body margin before modular-cable bend needs. Validate lead count, connector seating and safe bend radius physically.
- **Mains:** a 2200W PSU can approach or exceed the comfort margin of a 230V/10A circuit under sustained dual-GPU load once system overhead is included. Use a properly earthed dedicated circuit appropriate to the actual breaker/outlet and obtain electrician guidance rather than using adapters or overloaded strips.
- **Fans/support:** Track A uses 4x140 intake, 2x140 rear exhaust, 6x120 side GPU intake and AW420 top exhaust. Track B uses 3x140 bottom + 3x140 side intake, 2x140 rear exhaust and AIO top exhaust for slight positive pressure. Heavy GPUs require mechanical support after final orientation is chosen.
