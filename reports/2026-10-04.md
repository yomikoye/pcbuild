## Architecture advantages and compromises

Threadripper brings more PCIe connectivity, a superior multi-GPU platform, ECC RDIMM, quad-channel memory, higher capacity and stronger long-term expansion. AM5 lowers platform cost/power, improves gaming-focused CPU performance, offers cheaper board/RAM/case, white showcase design and substantial 128GB. AM5 tradeoffs: dual-channel, preferred 128GB, GPU x8/x8 electrical dual operation, fewer PCIe lanes, chipset/USB4 M.2 sharing, weaker 256GB+ expansion, and HAVN VGPU not two-GPU-ready as shipped. C keeps TRX50 expansion and two 24GB devices (48GB aggregate) at potentially lower *GPU acquisition* cost, permitting explicit distributed-model software or independent jobs; risks include used condition/warranty, separate VRAM, communication/software overhead, ~700W GPU heat, older Ampere Tensor/RT/media and no fit-validated matching pair. NVLink is optional and never transparently pools memory.
