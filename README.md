## Locked topology and safety notes

- Threadripper advantages: more PCIe connectivity, superior multi-GPU platform, ECC RDIMM, quad-channel memory, higher capacity and stronger expansion.
- AM5 advantages: lower platform price/power, better gaming-focused CPU, cheaper motherboard/RAM/case, white showcase design and substantial 128GB capacity.
- AM5 compromises: dual-channel memory, preferred 128GB configuration, PCIe 5.0 x8/x8 dual GPU, fewer lanes, chipset/USB4 M.2 sharing, weaker 256GB+ expansion and stock HAVN not two-GPU-ready.
- Track C advantages: Threadripper expansion, 48GB aggregate physical VRAM, lower acquisition cost and one distributed model or two independent 24GB workloads.
- Track C compromises: used-card/warranty risk, separate VRAM devices, software/communication overhead, approximately 700W GPU heat, older Ampere features and incomplete matching-pair evidence.
- Put 9100 PRO in `M2A_CPU` and 990 PRO in `M2C_SB`. `M2B_CPU` shares USB4; `M2D_SB` disables `PCIEX4`; `M2E_SB` is PCIe 4.0 x2.
- The 9950X3D2 requires BIOS F8 or newer. Mount PANORAMA's 55mm stack at the top as exhaust.
- Multiple GPUs do not automatically provide unified VRAM. NVLink is optional and does not create one transparent 48GB device.
