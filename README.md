### Important HAVN VGPU limitation

The selected HS 420 VGPU is designed for **one vertically mounted GPU**. Its included four-slot VGPU bracket and single PCIe 5.0 riser do not support two vertical cards. A future second RTX 5090 requires removing the VGPU installation and mounting both cards horizontally:

- Two RTX 5090 Founders Edition cards may fit within the six horizontal slots, subject to exact board spacing and airflow testing.
- Two 2.5-slot ProArt cards consume effectively all six horizontal slots and are not an airflow-safe default.
- Therefore B1 is **dual-GPU-power-ready**, not a claim that the stock case layout is dual-GPU-ready.

## Platform and compatibility differences

### Threadripper advantages

- Much more PCIe connectivity
- Superior multi-GPU chassis/platform strategy
- Quad-channel ECC RDIMM
- Higher future memory capacity
- Better expansion capability and long-term workstation flexibility

### AM5 advantages

- Much cheaper platform
- Better gaming-focused CPU
- Lower CPU power
- White showcase design with curved AMOLED cooler display and VGPU presentation
- 128GB remains substantial system memory

### AM5 compromises

- Dual-channel non-ECC UDIMM on the selected board
- Two CPU-fed GPUs operate PCIe 5.0 x8/x8, not x16/x16
- The selected VGPU case is one-GPU-oriented in its stock layout
- Fewer total PCIe lanes and less future memory expansion
- `M2B_CPU` shares bandwidth with ASMedia USB4
- `M2D_SB` disables the chipset `PCIEX4` slot
- `M2E_SB` is PCIe 4.0 x2

## Locked Track B implementation notes

- Exact motherboard: `X870E AORUS MASTER X3D ICE`, EAN `4719331876517`.
- 9950X3D2 requires BIOS F8 or newer; use the latest stable BIOS.
- One GPU operates PCIe 5.0 x16; two CPU-connected GPUs operate x8/x8.
- No documented M.2 connector reduces the CPU GPU pair.
- Put the 9100 PRO in `M2A_CPU` and the 990 PRO in `M2C_SB`.
- Exact cooler: white non-ARGB full PANORAMA 360, not PANORAMA SE. Its 55mm radiator/fan stack fits beneath HAVN's 65mm top limit; mount it top exhaust.
- Case airflow: 3x140 bottom intake, 3x140 right-side intake, 2x140 rear exhaust, and the AIO's 3x120 top exhaust. The case includes no fans.
- The 210mm PRIME PX-2200 fits the HAVN 220mm PSU-body limit with only 10mm nominal margin; cable-bend clearance must be checked.
- Two RTX 5090s remain separate 32GB devices; supported sharding or parallelism is required.
